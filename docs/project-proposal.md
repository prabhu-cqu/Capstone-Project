# SmartShop AI - Project Proposal

## Executive Summary

SmartShop AI is a proposed smart enterprise web application for a fictional small Australian retailer that sells computer, mobile and study accessories to students and young professionals.

The project addresses a practical retail problem: customers can struggle to interpret technical specifications, compare similar products and obtain timely assistance, while a small retailer may not have enough staff to answer routine questions at all times.

The proposed minimum viable product (MVP) combines established e-commerce functions with three controlled AI capabilities. Customers can browse, search and filter a product catalogue, view product details, manage a cart, complete a simulated checkout and review order history. The AI shopping assistant answers catalogue-related questions, a guided recommender matches stated needs to products stored in the catalogue, and review summaries help users understand common feedback. Administrators can manage products, stock information and order status.

The proposal deliberately excludes real payment processing, delivery-provider integration, large-scale hosting, demand forecasting and long-term behavioural profiling. This reduced scope keeps the solution achievable for three students within the academic term. Delivery will use an iterative twelve-week roadmap, rotating leadership, one accountable owner for each major deliverable, risk-based quality controls and measurable success criteria.
